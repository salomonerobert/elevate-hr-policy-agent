# Daily Meal Limit

- Individual meals on global business trips are capped at **US $120 per employee
  per day**. This is **not a per diem** — every meal must be individually detailed
  with receipts.

# Group Meals with Altostrat Colleagues

- Capped at **US $120 per employee per day**.
- List all attending colleagues in Concur for tax compliance.
- The **most senior colleague present** must pay and submit the expense, to ensure
  independent manager approval.

# High-Value Events

- Any group meal or customer-entertainment event costing **US $500 or greater per
  head** requires **written, pre-event VP approval**, attached to the Concur report.

# Non-Reimbursable

- Room movies, traffic/parking fines, and personal gifts are not reimbursable.
