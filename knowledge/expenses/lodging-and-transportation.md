# Lodging Caps

- Flight and hotel choices are strictly subject to **regional route caps**.

# Staying with Friends or Family

- If you stay with a friend or relative instead of a hotel, you may buy a **host
  gift of up to US $50 per day**, backed by **valid receipts**.
- **Cash or gift-card host gifts are strictly prohibited** — regardless of amount.
  (A $45 gift card is *not* reimbursable, because gift cards are prohibited even
  though the value is under the $50/day limit. Buy a physical gift with a receipt
  instead.)

# Ground Transport

- Prioritize public transit or shared rides. Personal car use is reimbursable on a
  per-distance basis in Concur (which covers fuel; separate fuel expenses are
  prohibited).
- Car rentals must be **midsize/intermediate or smaller**.
