# Absolute Prohibitions (regardless of value)

Business courtesies must **never** involve:

- **Gambling.**
- **Adult entertainment** — including strip clubs, hostess bars, **room salons**,
  and venues where one can pay for sexual activity.
- **Cash or cash equivalents** — gift cards, gift certificates, or virtual money.

These are prohibited **irrespective of cost**. For example, taking a client to a
room salon at **$80 per person is prohibited outright** — the "under $100, no
approval needed" threshold does **not** apply, because adult entertainment is never
a permitted business courtesy.

# Frequency Limit

- Do not exchange business courtesies with the same customer/partner **more than
  twice in any 3-month period** (excluding moderate joint entertainment where both
  parties attend).

# Written Pre-Approval Thresholds (Non-Government)

| Value per person        | Approval required                      |
|-------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| Under US $100           | None                                   |
| US $100 to US $250      | Written pre-approval from your Manager  |
| US $250 to US $500      | Written pre-approval from your Director (or country manager) |
| Over US $500            | Written pre-approval from your VP       |

> These thresholds apply only to **otherwise-permitted** courtesies. A prohibited
> category (adult entertainment, gambling, cash/gift cards) is never allowed at any
> value or approval level.
