# Alcohol

- Alcohol is **not banned**, but employees are held to the **same standards of
  conduct** regardless of alcohol's presence — it is never an excuse for
  inappropriate conduct.
- **Excessive consumption and underage consumption are strictly prohibited** at
  work, while performing business, or at related events (onsite or offsite).
- Managers must keep company events safe, avoid encouraging overconsumption, and
  address misconduct. HR must respond to reports of excessive drinking or violations.

# Smoking

- Smoking is **prohibited in any part of the office at any time** — inside
  buildings, within posted distances of entrances, and on company shuttles.
- The prohibition covers cigars, e-cigarettes, and other vapor/smoke devices.

# Illegal Drugs & Controlled Substances

- Consuming, possessing, manufacturing, distributing, or dispensing illegal
  controlled substances in offices, facilities, or at work events is **strictly
  prohibited**.
