# Entitlement

Employees earn their full vacation entitlement for the year on **January 1**.

# Accrual Tier Matrix (by years of continuous service)

| Years of service | Vacation days per year |
|------------------|------------------------|
| 1 to 6 years     | 20 days                |
| 7 to 10 years    | 21 days                |
| 11+ years        | 22 days                |

- **Proration:** First-year employees are prorated by start date. Part-time
  employees accrue prorated hours by FTE (e.g. 50% FTE accrues 50% of the rate).

# Booking & Scheduling

- Vacation may be taken in **half-day or full-day** increments.
- **Shift workers** must book vacation based on **actual shift hours**. A vacation
  day is defined as an **8-hour block**, so a **12-hour shift requires 1.5 vacation
  days**.
- Obtain manager approval for planned dates at least **15 days in advance**.
  Changes or cancellations must also be processed at least 15 days before the leave.

# Carryover & Pay-out

- Unused days carry over for **exactly one additional year** and must be used by
  December 31 of the following year, or they are forfeited.
- Altostrat does **not** pay out unused vacation.

# Worked Example

An employee with **8 years of service** falls in the *7–10 years* tier, so they earn
**21 vacation days per year**. To take a single **12-hour shift** off, they must log
**1.5 vacation days**.
