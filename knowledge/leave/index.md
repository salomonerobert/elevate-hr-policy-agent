# Leave & Time Off

Policies governing paid and unpaid time away from work.

- [Sick & Hospitalization Leave](/leave/sick-and-hospitalization-leave.md) — outpatient sick leave, hospitalization leave, and medical-certificate rules.
- [Vacation Leave](/leave/vacation-leave.md) — accrual tiers by years of service, proration, and booking rules (including shift workers).
- [Ramp-Back Time](/leave/ramp-back-time.md) — phased, paid return to work after long parental leave.
- [Bereavement Leave](/leave/bereavement-leave.md) — paid leave following the loss of a loved one.
- [Carer's Leave](/leave/carers-leave.md) — paid leave to care for a seriously or terminally ill loved one.
- [Unpaid & Personal Leave](/leave/unpaid-and-personal-leave.md) — unpaid time off and longer personal leave.
