# Purpose

Ramp-back time eases the transition back to work after a long leave.

# Eligibility

- You must have taken at least **10 consecutive weeks** of maternity, adoption,
  parental, or baby bonding leave.

# Duration, Hours & Pay

- Up to **2 weeks** of paid ramp-back time immediately upon return.
- During these 2 weeks you must work a **minimum of 50% of your normal weekly
  hours**, but you receive **100% of your normal salary**.

# Logging

- Salaried employees enter the hours **not worked** in **WorkWeek** under type
  "Ramp Back Time" with reason "Baby Bonding Leave".
- Hourly employees log this on their timecard in **gTime**.
