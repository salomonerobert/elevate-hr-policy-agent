# When It Applies

When accrued vacation is exhausted or low, employees may request unpaid leaves of
absence.

# Types

- **Unpaid Time Off (short-term):** With manager approval, up to **30 calendar
  days**.
- **Personal Leave (longer):** Up to **92 days**, subject to eligibility and
  approval (Handbook Section 18.3).
- **Unpaid Compassionate Leave:** Available per Handbook Section 18.4.

# Conditions During Leave

- Specific conditions govern benefits and systems access during extended leave
  (Handbook Section 18.5). Inappropriate use of leave may lead to disciplinary
  action (Section 18.6).
