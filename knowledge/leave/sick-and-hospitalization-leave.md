# Allowances

- **Outpatient sick leave:** Eligible employees and interns receive up to **14 days**
  of paid outpatient sick leave per calendar year, compensated at **100% of base
  salary**. Part-time and fixed-term employees are prorated by contracted hours.
- **Hospitalization leave:** An **additional 46 work days** of paid hospitalization
  leave per year. This is *not* an extension of outpatient sick leave — it is for
  inpatient stays, day surgeries, quarantine orders, or serious medical conditions
  certified by a practitioner at an approved hospital.

# Notification

- Notify your manager at least **one hour before your normal start time**. If your
  manager is unavailable, contact support.

# Medical Certificate (MC)

- If you are sick for **more than two work days**, you must submit an MC from a
  registered medical practitioner via **WorkWeek**.
- The MC must be submitted **within 48 hours** of taking the leave.
- If the MC is not submitted, Altostrat may decline paid sick leave or classify the
  absence as unpaid. Unapproved absences can result in disciplinary action.
