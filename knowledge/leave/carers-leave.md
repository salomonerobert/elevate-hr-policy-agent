# Allowance

- Up to **8 weeks of paid leave per loved one, per lifetime** (family member,
  partner, or dependent) to care for a seriously or terminally ill individual.

# Scheduling

- Counted in **work days**; may be requested in weekly blocks or daily schedules.
- Minimum duration is **half a work day**.

# Documentation

- You may be asked for written attestation or medical documentation verifying the
  serious health condition. Do **not** share sensitive medical details when
  contacting support.
