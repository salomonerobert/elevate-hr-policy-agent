# Allowance

- Up to **4 weeks (20 work days for a standard 5-day schedule) of paid leave per
  event**. This applies to the loss of a close loved one, including pregnancy loss
  (miscarriage or stillbirth).

# Timeline

- Bereavement leave must be taken **within 12 months of the death**.

# Exclusions

- Paid bereavement leave **does not apply to pet loss**. In those cases, arrange
  vacation, unpaid time off, or a flexible schedule with your manager.
